# BotTerminatorUpdates 🤖🚫

The official update source for the **Bot Terminator** Grok Bot: cleans bot-like, spammy and long-inactive followers off your X account (and, optionally, takes you off unwanted X lists). You review in rounds of 5 and decide; the bot does the removing itself, safely paced, verified and logged.

## Upgrade your Bot Terminator (for owners)
1. Copy this page's link: `https://github.com/EmpressHeavy/BotTerminatorUpdates`
2. Open your old Bot Terminator chat, paste the link, and say: **"upgrade yourself from this repo"**
3. Bot Terminator tells you what's new, takes a snapshot of your setup, and upgrades (this first upgrade is the only manual one).
4. It walks you through the setup cards again, pre-filled with your answers. Your Graveyard, Always Keep list, settings, history and memory are kept.

After that, updates are **automatic and always on**: once a week Bot Terminator checks this repo and, if there's a newer version, updates itself and sends you a short "Bot Terminator updated to vN, here's what's new" note. This is built in and can't be switched off (you can move the time it runs).

## Safety promises
- Bot Terminator only accepts updates from this exact repo.
- Update files can never make Bot Terminator remove, block or message anyone, post, sign in, or delete your data. Removals only happen in the normal review flow, under the mode and daily cap you chose.
- Your records are never overwritten. A snapshot is taken before every update and it rolls back if anything fails.
- An update never changes the optional settings you chose. New optional features start off and are offered to you as a card.
